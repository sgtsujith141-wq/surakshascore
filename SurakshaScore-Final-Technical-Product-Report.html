01 Executive Summary

SurakshaScore (Personal Security Score — PSS) is an on-device digital hygiene evaluation engine built for Indian mobile consumers, developers, and enterprise employees. It computes a transparent, explainable Device Hygiene Index (0–100) across five attack vectors: Device & OS Integrity, Apps & Permissions, Account Credential Security, Network Transport Safety, and Human Security Habits.

The system executes 100% locally with zero cloud telemetry, leveraging native OS keyguard APIs, package permission manifests, Web Crypto Shannon entropy, and $k$-anonymity breach inquiries to provide actionable security hardening with measurable score recovery.

02 & 03 Problem Statement & Proposed Solution
SIH Problem Statement:

Create a personal digital hygiene assessment system that empowers citizens to diagnose device risks, understand vulnerabilities in plain English, and remediate them without compromising privacy.

Deterministic Math No Opaque AI Hallucinations Scoring is pure math: 100 - Σ(penalty × confidence). Every point lost is mathematically traceable.
Hardware Keystore Zero-Cloud Privacy Passwords, vault entries, contacts, and personal data never leave the device.
Remediation Loop Point Recovery Simulator Translates complex security fixes into simple actions with instant score rewards (e.g. +16 pts).

08–13 Device Hygiene Index Scoring Engine Mathematics

CategoryScore[c] = max(0, min(100, 100 - Σ ( SeverityPenalty[f] × ConfidenceMultiplier[p] ) ))

HygieneIndex = round( Σ ( AdjustedWeight[c] × CategoryScore[c] ) )

47 Technical Q&A Defense for SIH Judges
Q: How does SurakshaScore evaluate passwords without storing or transmitting them?
A: We use client-side Shannon entropy calculation and $k$-anonymity via the SHA-1 algorithm. The app computes the SHA-1 hash locally using the Web Crypto API, extracts only the first 5 characters (prefix), and queries the Have I Been Pwned range API. The remaining 35 characters are matched client-side. The plaintext password and full hash never leave the device.
Q: How do you handle platform differences between Android and iOS without giving unfair scores?
A: When a category is blocked by the OS sandbox (such as querying other apps' permissions on iOS), our mathematical engine triggers Proportional Weight Redistribution. The unscoreable category's weight ($25\%$) is proportionally allocated across the remaining 4 scoreable vectors so that the total active weight always equals $1.0$, preventing unfair point deductions.
Q: Why did a Critical finding with a 40-point penalty only reduce the overall score by 10 points?
A: Deductions are applied to the Category Subscore (0–100), which is weighted at $25\%$ for Device Security. Reducing the category score from $100$ to $60$ results in a contribution of $60 \times 0.25 = 15.0\text{ pts}$ instead of $25.0\text{ pts}$, yielding an exact and mathematically consistent $-10\text{ pt}$ change to the overall Hygiene Index.
